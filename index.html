2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to your use of the App only. It does not cover:

  • Care you receive in person at the practice (that is covered by the practice's Notice of Privacy Practices).
  • Any third-party websites or services the App may link to.
  • ASA's main website (americansurgicalarts.com), which has its own separate privacy policy.

3. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address via Google Sign-In. We do not separately collect or store a password; authentication is handled by our sign-in provider.

Recovery information. To provide the App's core function, we store:

  • The procedure(s) associated with your account (e.g. Breast Augmentation, Abdominoplasty) and the performing doctor.
  • Your surgery date.
  • Drain output logs you enter, including the date, time of day (AM/PM), and amount in mL/cc.
  • Your notification preferences (e.g. whether you've enabled drain log reminders).

Device and technical information. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools. We do not currently use crash reporting tools.

Notifications. If you enable drain-log reminders, the App schedules local notifications directly on your device. These reminders do not transmit any additional data to us.

We do not:

  • Sell your personal information.
  • Use your information for advertising.
  • Collect precise location data.
  • Use AI tools to process your personal or health information.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • Provide the App's recovery-tracking features (e.g. saving your drain logs and showing your history).
  • Show recovery guidance relevant to your procedure.
  • Alert you to recovery milestones (for example, when your drain output has been low enough that you may be ready to contact the office).
  • Send reminders you have enabled.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your recovery or health information for marketing.

5. Important note about HIPAA

The recovery information in the App, including your procedure information and drain output logs, may be considered health information. The App's current infrastructure does not include HIPAA-specific safeguards or a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with our technology providers. We encourage you to avoid entering any information beyond what the App specifically asks for, and to contact our office directly for any information you consider especially sensitive.

We take reasonable security measures to protect your information (see Section 7), but you should be aware of this limitation when deciding what to enter into the App.

6. How we share your information

We share your information only as described here:

With the practice (ASA). At this time, your recovery information, including drain logs, is visible only to you within the App. It is not currently visible to ASA staff or your care team through the App itself. If you have concerns about your recovery, please contact the office directly.

With service providers. We use trusted third parties to run the App:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of your account and recovery data.
  • Google — used solely to let you sign in to your account (Google Sign-In). We do not use Google Analytics or advertising services within the App.

For legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of patients, the public, or ASA.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

7. How we store and protect your information

Your information is stored on Supabase's cloud infrastructure. We use safeguards intended to protect it, including:

  • Access controls that restrict each patient's data to that patient's account (enforced by row-level security), meaning no patient can view another patient's information.
  • Encryption of data in transit.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. How long we keep your information

We retain your recovery information (procedures and drain logs) for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, or request deletion, we will delete your associated recovery data. Some limited information may need to be retained if required by law.

9. Your rights and choices

You may:

  • Access the information we hold about you within the App at any time.
  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us, or by removing procedures within the App.
  • Turn reminders on or off at any time within the App, and disable notifications for the App in your device settings.

To request account deletion or ask about your data, contact us using the information in Section 1.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for patients who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will address it.

11. Third-party links and services

The App may reference third-party services (for example, tapping a phone number or email to contact the office opens your device's phone or mail app). Those apps and services have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. If changes are significant, we will notify you within the App. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
